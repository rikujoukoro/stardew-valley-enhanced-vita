everything game-related on their machine. Stardew Valley © ConcernedApe; this project is not affiliated with
ConcernedApe, Sickhead Games or Sony.

vitaGL is used under LGPL-3.0 with these steps (`vpk/pack-toolchain.sh` and `vpk/pack-toolchain-zip.sh` do them):
- the toolchain archive carries, next to `libvitaGL.a`, the LGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 texts and the complete source of
  commit 76f43e6, plus our edits to it (`patch-vitagl.py`, a modified version per LGPL-3.0 section 2) and the exact
  build script and options (`build-vitagl.sh`), all under `licenses/` (`/root/vita/licenses/` in the WSL archive);
- the VPK is linked from object files on the player's machine, so anyone can rebuild vitaGL (changed or not) and
  relink (section 4d0);
- the VPK's own code is not under the LGPL; vitaGL's notice is in this file and in the archive.
